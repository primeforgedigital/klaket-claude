# Klaket Studio

The full [Klaket](https://klaket.io) AI marketing studio inside Claude: video ads, social posts and landing pages for your products, from one conversation.

## What it does

- **Video ads**: 4 to 15-second videos from a product photo or an idea — with an AI presenter or scenes only, voiceover and burned-in captions, in 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9. Klaket shows you the scene and the cost first; the video is only made after you approve.
- **Social posts**: 4 posts around your real product photos, with caption, hashtags and ad copy.
- **Landing pages**: hosted at klaket.io, with a lead form, WhatsApp button or store link, published when you ask.
- **Launch kit**: video, posts and landing page for the same product.

Works in English, Spanish and Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil).

## Use it

1. Add the plugin and connect **Klaket** from the plugin's Connectors tab. You sign in on klaket.io and click **Authorize**.
2. Ask Claude, for example: *"Make a 10-second TikTok video ad for my running shoes"* or *"Launch kit for my new candle: video, posts and a landing page"*.

Creating uses the credits of your Klaket account, at the same price as on klaket.io, and needs an account with a Klaket plan.

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to your own Klaket account through https://klaket.io/mcp, after you sign in and authorize it on klaket.io (OAuth). Claude sends Klaket only what each request needs — the product, the idea, the options and, if you give them, public links to product photos — and receives the result: your credits, your products and the posts, pages and videos you create. Klaket never receives your password, payment details or your conversations with Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing. Klaket's privacy policy: https://klaket.io/en/legal/privacy. Disconnect any time in Klaket → Settings → Connected assistants.
