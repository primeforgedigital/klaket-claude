# Klaket

Social media posts and landing pages for your products, made by [Klaket](https://klaket.io) from a conversation with Claude.

## What it does

- **Social posts**: a batch of 4 posts designed around your real product photos (feed, 4:5 and story sizes), with headline, button, caption, hashtags and ad copy.
- **Landing pages**: a complete page for your product, hosted at klaket.io, with a lead form, a WhatsApp button or a link to your store. It stays private until you ask Claude to publish it.
- **Launch kit**: posts and a landing page for the same product in one go.

Works in English, Spanish and Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil).

## Use it

1. Add the plugin and connect **Klaket** from the plugin's Connectors tab. You sign in on klaket.io and click **Authorize**.
2. Ask Claude, for example: *"Create 4 Instagram posts for my vitamin C serum"* or *"Build a landing page for my shop with a WhatsApp button and publish it"*.
3. Save a product once (Claude can create it from public photo links) and reuse it every time.

Creating uses the credits of your Klaket account, at the same price as on klaket.io, and needs an account with a Klaket plan.

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to your own Klaket account through https://klaket.io/mcp/claude, after you sign in and authorize it on klaket.io (OAuth). Claude sends Klaket only what each request needs — the product, the idea, the options and, if you give them, public links to product photos — and receives the result: your credits, your products and the posts, pages you create. Klaket never receives your password, payment details or your conversations with Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing. Klaket's privacy policy: https://klaket.io/en/legal/privacy. Disconnect any time in Klaket → Settings → Connected assistants.
